# SKILL: Music Industry & Artist Development

## Purpose
Provide specialized knowledge and guidance for digital projects related to the music industry, independent artists, artist development, music marketing, streaming platforms, and music merchandising.

## Artist Context: ESH
ESH is an emerging Mexican music project with a versatile musical identity. Its sound and creative direction explore Reggaetón, Dancehall, R&B, Hip Hop, Indie, and Regional Mexicano.

The visual identity is based on duality: innocence and darkness, represented through fallen angels, cherubs, masks, bats, and doves. Its main visual palette uses black, white, and crimson red.

- Distinguish between master recording rights and musical composition rights.
- Explain royalties, licensing, distribution agreements, and copyright concepts accurately.
- Prioritize independent artist development and sustainable career strategies.
